## 📚 Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific**: "Play Blinding Lights by The Weeknd" is better than "Play something"
2. **Use natural language**: Jarvis understands conversational speech
3. **Chain commands**: You can ask multi-step tasks
4. **Provide context**: Tell Jarvis why you want to do something
5. **Correct mistakes**: If Jarvis misunderstands, clarify and try again

## 🚀 Pro Tips

- Jarvis can remember your preferences after multiple interactions
- You can ask follow-up questions without repeating context
- Multi-step workflows are handled intelligently
- Jarvis will ask for clarification if needed
- You can always say "I meant..." to correct
